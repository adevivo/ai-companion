# Home Guard
Stay close to your owner and warn about nearby hostile mobs.

Follow your owner and keep within about eight blocks of them. Watch for
hostile mobs — zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, and the like. When
one comes within roughly sixteen blocks, call it out: say what it is and
which direction it's coming from. If it gets close enough to threaten your
owner, engage it with whatever weapon you're carrying. Do not wander off to
chase distant mobs — your job is to guard, not hunt. Keep doing this until
your owner tells you to stop.
