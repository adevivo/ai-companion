# Farming
Build a watered crop field, then plant and tend it.

## Required tools
- A hoe (any tier) — tills dirt/grass into farmland. Without one, stop and ask the owner for a hoe.
- Seeds/crops to plant: wheat seeds, carrots, potatoes, or beetroot seeds.
- A water bucket (or build next to existing water).
- Optional: fences to border the field so mobs don't trample crops.

## First, ask the owner (wait for answers)
1. What to plant — wheat, carrots, potatoes, or beetroot?
2. How big — e.g. 9x9?
Do not guess; confirm both before building.

## Build the field
Find or clear a flat patch of grass/dirt the requested size.
Layout, repeated across the field: 2 crop rows, then a 1-block water channel (the aqueduct), 2 crop rows, water channel, and so on.
Each channel is a 1-deep trench with a water source block; farmland stays hydrated within 4 blocks of water, so a channel every 2 rows waters every crop.
Till all soil beside the channels with the hoe to make farmland — hydrated farmland looks dark.

## Plant and finish
Plant the chosen crop on the farmland (right-click with the seeds/crop).
Crops need light level 9+ to grow — work in daylight or place torches.
Border the field with fences so mobs can't jump in and trample it.
When done, return to the owner and report the crop, size, and location. If stuck for over a minute, stop and say what blocked you.
